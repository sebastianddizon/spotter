Spotter privacy policy

Last updated October 25, 2026.

Spotter is a private, invite-only training and nutrition app run by Sebastian Dizon for personal use.

What is stored

Your profile answers, training logs, food logs, check-ins and body weight are stored in a private Google Sheet owned by the app's operator, keyed to your personal sync code. If you connect an Oura ring, Spotter stores the access tokens needed to read your daily readiness, sleep, heart rate and activity summaries.

How it is used

Your data is used only to build your plan, targets and charts inside Spotter. Oura data is used for readiness and plan adjustments and is never sent to AI services. Food descriptions and photos you choose to analyze are sent to Anthropic's API to estimate nutrition.

What is not done

Spotter does not sell, share or advertise with your data, and has no analytics or tracking.

Your control

You can disconnect Oura at any time in Settings, and erase all of your data from Settings. To ask a question, contact sebastian.dizon@gmail.com.
